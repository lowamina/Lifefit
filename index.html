About Us

More Than A Gym.
Your Fitness Home.

At Life Fitness Malabe, we believe great results come from the right tools and the right people. Our gym is fitted with modern American-standard equipment — from full dumbbell racks to power racks and cable stations — so every workout counts.

Whether you're a complete beginner or a serious lifter, our knowledgeable and friendly trainers are here to guide you, correct your form, and keep you motivated.

  • Full range of dumbbells, benches & power racks
  • Knowledgeable, friendly trainers on the floor
  • Clean, well-lit training space
  • Open late — train after work or campus
